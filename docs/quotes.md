## How each quote was verified
- **Greek (`el`)**: extracted programmatically from the Perseus canonical-greekLit TEI files (`tlg0086.tlg010/025/034/035/038 .perseus-grc2.xml`), then re-checked as present on the linked Perseus Hopper page. The Bekker page+column comes from the `<milestone unit="page">` that precedes the clause. Chapter numbers come from Perseus's section structure (Politics: Jowett's Part II, Politics has no chapters in Perseus). Modifier apostrophes (ʼ) normalised to ’.
- **English (`en`)**: each fragment is an exact substring of the Internet Classics Archive (MIT) text of the public-domain translation named in the table, checked by script. Edits are limited to: capitalising the first letter, trimming the clause, and a leading `…` where the clause starts mid-sentence. No paraphrase.
- Metaphysics book numbers: Ross's II is Bekker's α (little alpha), XII is Λ.
- Not given: Bekker line numbers (page+column only, as in the brief). All English is ≤140 characters (max 134).
- Translators: Ross 1908 (NE, Metaphysics); Jowett 1885 (Politics); Butcher 1895 (Poetics); Rhys Roberts 1924 (Rhetoric). MIT's pages name the translator, not the edition year; years are the standard first-publication/Oxford-Works dates.

## Not added (could not verify, or unsuitable)
- Politics VIII.3 "the first principle of all action is leisure" (1337b): genuine in content, but no Jowett text with Greek could be checked here (MIT's Politics file stops at Book II; Gutenberg's Politics is a different translation). Verify before adding.
- Nicomachean Ethics IX.9, 1169b "a friend, being another self, furnishes what a man cannot provide by his own effort": it is the objectors' view that Aristotle goes on to answer, so it is not his claim.
- Nicomachean Ethics II.9 "anyone can get angry — that is easy": genuine (1109a26) but not in the set; the popular "Anybody can become angry…" is a paraphrase.
- Poetics 24, 1460a: "the poet should prefer probable impossibilities to improbable possibilities" — genuine (Butcher), not in the set.
- Posterior Analytics, De Anima, Parts of Animals: not verified in this pass (Greek files not located in time); no quotes included.
