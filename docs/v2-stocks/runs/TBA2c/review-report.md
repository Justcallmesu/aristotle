## Findings
- **MINOR-1 — the pick is also dropped by Back, resume and rotation, not only by an open.** `pageVisible(true)` forgets the pick on every false→true edge (`BriefViewModel.kt:121`), and `LifecycleResumeEffect` fires on resume and on re-entering composition (`BriefScreen.kt:146-149`). So yesterday's brief → tap a focus stock → Back lands on today's brief. Coming back from a news link in the browser, or rotating the phone, does the same. The data stays correctly labelled (chip and subline) and one tap recovers, so this is deferrable. `BriefPickTest`'s re-entry test codifies it. **Fix:** forget only in `opened()`, which every tap route already calls; drop the reset from `pageVisible`; change the test to `opened()` → `pageVisible(true)`.
- **MINOR-2 — the ticker turns a stale `pending` into a false "No brief today".** `latest` is read only on a visible edge (`BriefViewModel.kt:109-110`), and the 5 s follow runs only while `running` (`:174`). If Home or the page stays on screen from before the slot until 09:00, the card flips from "Comes at 08:30" to "No brief today", even though the brief was written at 08:30 and its notification rang. This needs about 30 minutes of screen-on idle, so it is rare. **Fix:** reload `latest` when the clock passes `at`/09:00 while `pending`, or when a `brief` alert lands in `stockAlerts.feed`.
- **MINOR-3 — attack item 4: the `404 no_brief` page contradicts the card.** The page shows the static `stocks_brief_none` "The first one comes at 08:30 on a trading day" and ignores `status` (`BriefScreen.kt:190`). With the brief job off (and once its 7 kept days have aged out, permanently), the card reads "the brief is off" and the page promises 08:30. With the job time changed, the time is wrong. `stocks_brief_page_title` "08:30 brief" is a feature name: a NIT, or rename to "Morning brief". **Fix:** in that branch, show `briefCardLine(status)` and format `stocks_brief_none` with `briefAt`.
- **NIT:** `stockPostedAt` right after `notify` can miss a post that NMS has not enqueued yet, so a millisecond-apart burst may ring twice; an in-process last-posted map per tag would cover it. In ID, the job time reads "pukul 08:30" next to "09.00". `late` uses today's job time for an older day. `take` → `notify` is still a non-suspending window before the second tray sweep (no realistic path).

VERDICT: APPROVED (no BLOCKER/MAJOR; MINOR-1..3 deferrable, MINOR-1 is the cheapest and most visible)
