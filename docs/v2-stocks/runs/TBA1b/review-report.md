## Findings
- **MINOR-1: `priceLike` refuses ordinary year text that the prompt invites (brief.go:636 vs :780-844).** The prompt says "no number above 99 (years excepted)". The validator, however, refuses a year without a year word or month next to it. Probes: refused: `sepanjang 2026`, `akhir/awal/hingga 2026`, `in 2026`, `outlook 2026`, `H1 2026`, `kuartal III 2026`, `semester I 2026`, `2026F`, `6/11/2026`, `MA 200`. Passed: `tahun 2026`, `Q3 2026`, `FY2025`, `5 November 2026`, `2026-11-05`, `150%`, `naik 12 persen`, `MA200`, `RSI 14`, `1.5x`, `6/11`. Prices are all refused: `support 2.050`, `di 2050`, `Rp4.900`, `Rp.2050`, `IDR 2050`, `4,9 ribu`, `5rb`. Risk: news-driven briefs often cite years, so expect frequent repair turns (a second Opus turn). An actual `invalid_brief` needs the repair reply to fail as well, and the repair names the number, so most briefs should still land. Fix: state the validator's year rule in the prompt ("a year only as `tahun 2026`, a month + year or Q1-Q4/FY + year"), and/or accept the common ID/EN year prepositions and `H1`/`H2`/`semester`/`kuartal` before a year.
- **MINOR-2: two small holes in `priceLike`, and §20.4 does not describe them.** (a) Two bare years in a hyphen range pass as dates: `range 2010-2050` → nothing refused (:842-843 accepts `-<digit>` after a year). (b) Any letters glued to the number pass, not only indicator names: `level2050` → nothing refused (:789). §20.4 (protocol.md:1520-1521) says "an indicator name (MA200)". Fix: accept the hyphen form only as a full `YYYY-MM-DD`, and only `MA|EMA|SMA|RSI|FY|LQ|IDX|Q|H` as glued prefixes.
- **NIT-1 (doc):** the §20.4 POST order lists `400 bad_request` last, but the body is checked first and `setLang` is applied even when the POST is then refused (brief.go:910-929, protocol.md:1532-1533). §20.4 also does not say that automatic runs are off while `stocks-alerts.json` is unusable, or that a model mismatch fails the job `model_error` and counts against the cap.
- **NIT-2:** a save that fails at run time (file loaded fine, write fails) leaves `persist=true`. Automatic runs then go on with an unsaved counter, and a restart allows 5 more (alerts.go:168-175, :551-555). The brief only asked for `persist=false`; a fix is to have `saveLocked` return its error and stop the runs on a failed counter save.
- **NIT-3 (shape):** the brief-failure block appears twice in `runJob` (brief.go:295-299, :307-311). The "delete, else warn, then forget" pattern now appears three times (analyzer.go:530-537, :544-554, brief.go:390-396). Extract `failJob` and `deleteSession` helpers.

A settings PUT in the evening followed by a manual EOD scan upgrades the re-rank keys to `eod`. That can start a Sonnet run at night from unchanged data.
