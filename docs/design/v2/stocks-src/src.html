Aristotle — Stocks board (TD4, slice 1)

Slice 1 of the IDX stocks work (Q84): a watchlist, holdings you type in with P/L after broker fees (Q88), price charts and a news feed, behind a Chats | Stocks switch on Home (Q89). Same v2 language as mockups.html: palette A, Soft corners, grouped rows, Home C, Literata titles + Atkinson Hyperlegible Next / Mono, M3 Compose parts only. Every screen is drawn at 393 × 873 dp, light and dark. Notes and the Compose mapping: docs/design/v2/stocks-notes.md.

Samples, not quotes. Prices, price series, volumes, holdings and headlines are made up for the board, but they follow the real rules: IDX tick sizes (1 / 2 / 5 / 10 / 25 by price band), lots of 100 shares, Thursday sessions (S1 09:00–12:00, S2 13:30–15:49, close 16:15), Yahoo's ~10 min delay, fees 0,15 % buy / 0,25 % sell, and the RSS sources the PM probed (CNBC Indonesia, Kontan, IDX Channel). Only fields the planned sources carry are drawn: Yahoo chart gives last, previous close, day open/high/low, volume and the 52-week range; RSS gives source, title and time. No P/E, market cap, foreign flow or bid/offer. Taps follow the primary path; on the board they jump to the target screen, in Clickable path they navigate one phone. Touch the chart (or drag over it) for the crosshair.

Clickable path

  1. Home: tap Stocks in the switch (and back to Chats).
  2. Stocks home: tap a watchlist row, the holdings card, a news card, News / Holdings in the bottom bar, or + (add).
  3. Detail: change range and candles / line, drag on the chart, Edit the holding, Open in broker app.
  4. Holdings: tap a row to edit, Add holding for the sheet.
  5. News: filter All / Watchlist / Holdings; tap a ticker chip.

All Tweaks apply here. Theme "Light + dark" shows light.
