Aristotle icon concepts

Draft for T20 (spec Q6, Q29). Three ways to redraw the reference engraving as an adaptive icon. Each is shown at 48, 96 and 192 px (48 dp is the launcher size to beat), on light and dark launchers, and as the Android 13+ themed (monochrome) icon.

Reference: docs/assets/icon-reference.png. All three were traced over it, so the proportions come from the engraving, not from memory. Ink #2B2119 on parchment #F3EAD8 (palette A).

A Seated on the plinth

The whole engraving as one silhouette: hunched back, hand on chin, crossed arm, the block under him. White cuts carry the arm and the folds.

Most faithful to the reference and the most recognisable shape at 48 dp. The face itself disappears below 96 px; the pose does the work.

B Hand on chin

Head, fist and forearm only, in line art. The gesture the brief describes, cropped tight.

Weakest of the three: at 48 dp the fist reads as a blob, and line art thins out on dark launchers. Kept to show the line-art direction.

C Coin head

The head from the engraving struck on a coin, like an Attic silver drachm. Headband instead of the curls.

Cleanest at 48 dp and as a monochrome glyph. Loses the hand-on-chin gesture, which is the thing that says "thinking".

Notes for T20

  • Foreground and background are separate layers. The parchment background is a flat colour; no texture, because texture turns to noise at 48 px.
  • Cut lines in A are drawn in the background colour here. In the real foreground and monochrome layers they must be holes (path subtraction), so the themed icon shows the launcher's colour through them. The -monochrome.svg files already do this with a mask.
  • Nothing important sits outside the 66 dp safe zone, so circle, squircle and rounded-square launcher masks all keep the figure.
  • The colours used for the themed preview are a plausible Material You pair, not the phone's real wallpaper colours.
