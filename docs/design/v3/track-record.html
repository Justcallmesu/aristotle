Aristotle — AI track record board (TD6)

Scores every analyzer verdict against what the price did afterwards (spec Q116–Q120). Same v2 stocks language as screener.html: palette A, Soft corners, grouped rows, Literata titles, Atkinson Hyperlegible, green / red text for gain / loss, M3 Compose parts only. 393 × 873 dp, light and dark, EN and ID (Tweaks). Notes and the Compose mapping: docs/design/v3/track-record-notes.md.

Surfaces. The track record page is a Compare surface (one row per verdict type, same measures, read down the page); the verdict-card line and the Past calls list are small Monitor additions to the stock page; the scoring sheet is Learn. No hero, no stat tiles.

Samples, not results. Codes are real IDX tickers; every verdict, outcome, percent and count below is generated for the board (deterministic, 12 Oct – 5 Nov 2026) and follows the spec rules. They say nothing about the real AI. The numbers come from one sample list, so filters in the clickable path recompute them. Open choices (marked PM): minimum 10 verdicts per type, result base = top of the entry zone, rate denominator = decided calls. See the report, "Needs the user".

Picks for the user (recommended one first, both are Tweaks): 1 summary as cards · table · 2 result chips as tonal fill · outline with icon.

Clickable path

  1. Stocks home: tap ⋮ (bottom bar) → AI track record.
  2. Or tap the ADRO row → the verdict card → its AI record line.
  3. Page: the three filter chips open menus and recompute the table; ⓘ opens how scoring works.
  4. Stock page: Past calls shows 3, Show more expands.

Scenario in Tweaks: no history, only today's verdicts, PC offline. Theme "Light + dark" shows light.
